Who I Am & What I Do

A passionate IT student focused on cybersecurity, cloud technologies, and building practical solutions that make a real difference.

"Lifelong Learning Principle"

Technology advances constantly. Success comes from adapting quickly and maintaining unwavering discipline under pressure.

I am a 4th-year Information Technology student and consistent Dean's Lister at St. Dominic College of Asia (GPA: 94.83). I am also certified as an Information Technology Specialist in Cybersecurity through Pearson VUE and Certiport. My passion for technology began with curiosity about how computers and networks communicate, and has grown into a drive to build secure, reliable, and user-friendly systems.

My hands-on technical work spans Cybersecurity, Cloud Fundamentals, and IoT Smart Devices. I have built functional hardware prototypes — including the Rx Box smart pill dispenser and an automated waste sorting system — alongside mobile applications and web platforms designed to help people in their everyday routines.

Beyond technical projects, I actively serve in student leadership as a DominixiCode Officer and Academic Department Representative, helping organize workshops, student assemblies, and peer study groups. Combined with my customer service and cashier experience in retail, I bring patience, strong communication, and the ability to stay calm and detail-oriented under pressure.

Continuous Growth & Future Goals

Backed by 18 Cisco Networking Academy credentials, DICT trainings, and hands-on competition experience at the CALABARZON Regional HackForGov, I am actively expanding my knowledge in cloud platforms (such as Firebase, Oracle Cloud, and AWS fundamentals) to prepare for a successful career in cloud computing and cybersecurity.
